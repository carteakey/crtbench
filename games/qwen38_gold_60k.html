Momentum

Acceleration, friction, skid-turn and a dash speed that carries. Jump velocity scales with horizontal speed, and holding the button halves gravity mid-rise.

Tile solver

Axis-separated AABB resolution against a 15-row grid. Head-bumps resolve to the nearest column, bump-animate, kill what stands on top, and spit out items.

Chiptune

Every blip and both music tracks are synthesised live with oscillators and noise buffers — no sample files, no assets, no requests.

Three courses

Overworld, underground and a dusk sky-garden with moving platforms. Each has its own palette, parallax layer and soundtrack.
